Hello from QNX on the Raspberry Pi 4B!

This short note is a plain text file. encclient splits it into Data Blocks,
encserver encrypts them with AES-256-CTR on its Worker Pool, and the result
is an Encrypted File: a readable header followed by encrypted bytes.

Decrypt it again with:   ./encclient -d hello.txt.enc
and you get this exact text back, byte for byte.
